Nicotine Craving and Anxiety Dynamics

Power BI capstone project. I led a 20 person team.

When do nicotine cravings and anxiety peak during recovery, and what is linked to them? The goal was to give rehabilitation participants and counsellors patterns they can plan around.

Data
Kaggle dataset: 30,000 daily records (1,000 participants × 30 days), 35 columns
Tools
Excel, Power BI Desktop, Power Query, DAX
Output
3-page interactive dashboard and a written project report

What I found

  • Afternoons are the peak. Average craving was 6.33 in the afternoon, against 5.54 in the morning.
  • Stress days stand out. Days with a major stress event averaged 7.12 craving, against 5.94 on other days.
  • Stronger cravings last longer. High-intensity cravings ran about 76 minutes on average, low-intensity ones about 40.
  • Resisting works about half the time. 47.6% of resist attempts succeeded, falling from 53% for low-intensity cravings to 44% for high-intensity ones.
  • Craving and anxiety rise together. The scatter plot shows a positive relationship. Over the 30 days, both stayed flat.

What I did

  • Cleaned and formatted the data in Excel and Power Query: standardised labels, filled missing numbers with the median and missing categories with the most frequent value.
  • Modelled it as a galaxy schema with 2 fact tables, 3 dimension tables and a summary table.
  • Wrote DAX measures for average craving, anxiety, stress, sleep and resist success rate.
  • Built the dashboard and turned the results into recommendations: extra support in the afternoon, a coping plan for stress days, and starting a coping step as soon as a craving begins.
  • Stated the limits plainly: the data is observational, so the findings are associations, not causes.

Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard

Individual project. Excel and Power BI.

How is a three-store coffee shop performing, and which products, hours and months drive its sales? I turned the sales data into a two-page dashboard that supports business decisions.

Data
Coffee shop sales: 149K transactions, 214K units sold
Tools
Excel, Power BI, Power Query, DAX
Output
2-page interactive dashboard with store, category and product filters

What I found

  • $698.81K in revenue came from 149K transactions, at an average unit price of $3.38.
  • Coffee leads. It brought in $269.95K (38.63%). With tea at 28.11%, the two make up about two-thirds of sales.
  • Stores are close. Hell’s Kitchen was highest at $236.51K, with Astoria and Lower Manhattan within about 3% of it.
  • Mornings sell most. 9 AM–12 PM was the busiest period and 6–9 PM the slowest.
  • Sales grew through the half-year. From a dip in February, monthly sales rose every month to about $166K in June.

What I did

  • Cleaned and prepared the sales data in Excel and Power Query.
  • Wrote DAX measures for total sales, quantity sold, transactions and average unit price.
  • Built store, hourly, category, monthly, day-of-week and product views, with slicers for store, product type and date.
  • Added a navigation panel and a consistent coffee-themed layout so the two pages read as one report.
